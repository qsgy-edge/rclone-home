rclone-readonly

rclone-readonly is a personal-use instance of the open-source command-line tool rclone. Its single purpose is to let its owner synchronize their own files between their personal Google Drive account and their own private servers, in read-only mode, for backup and browsing. The app has no other purpose, no other users, and offers no services to the public.

Purpose of this application

  • Connect to a Google Drive account owned by the user who grants consent.
  • Read and list files from that account onto the user's own machines.
  • No data is collected, stored, or shared by the app operator; files are transferred directly between Google's servers and the user's own machine.

Compliance with Google APIs Terms of Service

This application does not use any Google API or any data obtained from Google APIs to generate or distribute AI-generated intimate imagery, non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII), or any other prohibited content. It accesses Google Drive solely for personal file synchronization as described above.
